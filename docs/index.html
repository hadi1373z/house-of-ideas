Working in the app today

A house you can make your own.

01 / SPACE

Design the rooms.

Draw and resize rooms, change their themes, and connect them with doors. Build the layout into a walkable house.

02 / MEMORY

Make ideas tangible.

Attach notes to crystals, rings, spheres, or books. Edit them, move them between rooms, and keep them saved.

03 / PERSPECTIVE

Find another view.

Use the joystick to walk inside, or step back into dollhouse view. Give related thoughts a place near one another.
